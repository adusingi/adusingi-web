I did not have a business idea.

I had the same photo on two websites. I had uploaded it twice.

This is the second post in the series. [The first one](/blog/free-website-with-cloudflare-claude-code) showed how to put a website online. This one is about what to build.

## The First Problem

I build web applications. Some of them need photos.

So every time, I did the same thing. Look for the photo on my computer. Try to remember where I saved it. Upload it. Get a link.

Then the same photo had to go on another site. So I uploaded it again. Same photo. Second upload. Second link.

One day I stopped and looked at it. This is not serious, I said. Why do I not have one place for my photos?

So I built one.

**files.mobayilo.com.** I upload a photo once. I get a link. I use that link on any site I want. If I upload the same photo again, I get the same link back.

That is all it does. One place. One link.

## The Second Problem

That worked. For a while.

Then one day I visited a place. I took photos. I liked them. I opened Instagram and posted them there and then.

I came home. I wanted the same photos on my portfolio.

And I saw the work waiting for me. Upload the photos. Then open my portfolio. Then put the same photos there too.

I had already posted them once, on Instagram. Now I had to do it two more times.

No.

## The Small Fix

Not every photo belongs on my portfolio. Only some of them.

So I added one thing. When I upload a photo, I can mark it. This one goes on the portfolio.

That is the whole feature. A mark.

The photo then appears on my site, straight from my central photo library. I do not open the code. I do not touch the site at all.

👉 [See the result on my photography page](/photography)

## Why I Tell You This

There was no plan. There was no big idea.

The first tool came from one small annoyance. The second came from using the first tool.

You do not need to be an entrepreneur. You do not need a problem that everybody has. You need one problem that you have today.

Solve that one. Then use what you built. The next problem shows itself.

It is also more fun than doing the same steps again and again.
