I am starting a series about getting something real on the internet.

Building has never been as easy as it is right now, and most people have not noticed yet. Every post in this series takes one thing and shows every step. Nothing skipped, nothing assumed.

This is the first one: **how to make a website for free with Cloudflare, using Codex or Claude Code.** No terminal. No credit card. Four things to copy and paste.

## What You Need

An AI coding assistant on your computer — Claude Code or Codex, either one — and a web browser. That is the whole list. You do not need a Cloudflare account yet. The first step makes one for you.

## The Four Steps

1. **Get ready.** Your assistant checks what your computer is missing, installs it, and opens Cloudflare's sign-in page in your browser.
2. **Create.** It asks what to call your site and makes the folder.
3. **Design.** It asks one question — what is this site about? — builds the page, and changes whatever you want changed. In plain language. No jargon.
4. **Publish.** It puts the site online and hands you a real web address.

Each step is one line you copy and paste. Your assistant reads the instructions and does the work.

## What It Costs

Nothing. Cloudflare's free tier takes no card, and there is nothing to cancel.

## Does It Actually Work?

Here is the one I made while testing it, a one-day itinerary for Ushimado, the town I live near:

👉 [ushimado-day-trip.aimabled.workers.dev](https://ushimado-day-trip.aimabled.workers.dev)

Here is the prompt: "I just want a list of things to do in Ushimado for one day." It picked a name and carried on.

One thing it could not do was sign in to Cloudflare for me. It opened the page and stopped, and I clicked Authorize. That part stays with you, and it should.

## Why This Matters

The distance between "I have an idea" and "it is on the internet" is much shorter than most people believe. What stands in the way is rarely ability. It is vocabulary — repository, framework, deploy, DNS — a wall of words that makes capable people decide this is not for them.

The people who do this for a living use the same tools, in the same order, for the same reasons. Nobody handed me that vocabulary, and I did not need it.
