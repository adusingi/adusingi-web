Working with AI is not improvisation.

AI is a very powerful tool — if you know how to work with it, and if you challenge it.

## The Default

I asked for an architecture for a small SaaS. The model gave me Next.js. Good framework. Wrong answer here.

The product is almost all backend: inbound email, background jobs, LLM calls, reminders, outbound email, and a simple dashboard. Ruby on Rails does all of that in one language, one process, one container.

## The Question

So I asked one question:

**"Why not Ruby on Rails?"**

The first line of the answer is the whole lesson:

> "You are right to ask. I defaulted to TypeScript from habit."

## The Real Skill

The model did not fail. It gave me the most common pattern in its training data. That is what a default is.

Your job is not to accept the default. Your job is to know enough to ask the second question.

That is the real skill. Not prompting. Judgement.
