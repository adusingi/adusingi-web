I forgot a meeting.

I only found out because the other person wrote to cancel it.

This is the third post in the series. The first two were about [getting online](/blog/free-website-with-cloudflare-claude-code) and [choosing what to build](/blog/i-did-not-have-a-business-idea). This one is about the week I could no longer hold it all.

## Too Many Lives

I run an academy. I screen the students. I prepare the cohorts. I write the lessons.

I build web applications.

I have a day job. Real tasks, real deadlines. I work remotely, so nothing tells me where the job stops and my own business starts.

And I have a family. My youngest is at school, and I pick her up. Her timetable changes. A new one arrives every month, because at her age the week is full of activities.

Every one of these sends me something. An email. A booking. A message.

None of it was in one place. So I kept it in my head.

## The Day It Failed

Then came the meeting I forgot.

I was lucky. The other person cancelled it, and that message is the only reason I ever knew.

This is not serious, I said. I need to see my day before the day starts.

## What I Wanted

Not an inbox. Not another task app. Not a calendar.

One page. I open it in the morning. It answers one question:

What must I not miss today?

So I built one.

## What It Is

**Clarity Board.**

I connect the things I already use. My mail accounts. My calendars. My booking page.

It gives me one page for today. My meetings, and the things that need an answer from me.

That was the whole first version.

👉 [See Clarity Board](https://inbox.mobayilo.com)

## Then I Used It

And the board filled up with mail. All of it.

A second copy of my inbox helps nobody. That is what I was already failing at.

So I added the screener. Nothing reaches my board until I say yes to the sender. I say it once. Everybody else waits at the door.

My board is not my mail. It is the short list of what I must not miss.

Newsletters came next. I like them, but they are not tasks. So they got their own room, with a summary I can ask for.

Then a chat, so I can ask what is waiting and say remind me of this.

## Why I Tell You This

There was no plan. There was no big idea.

I had one bad morning and one problem I could name. I built the smallest thing that answered it. Then I used that thing, and it showed me the next three.

You do not need an idea nobody has had. You need one problem you have today.
